Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

Chapter1 is operated by Bellaworks Web Design ("we"). This policy describes what we collect and what we do with it. The short version: we collect what the app needs to work, we don't sell it, and you can export or delete everything yourself.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address, display name, and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Your library: the books you log — titles, authors, statuses, ratings, dates, and notes.
  • Billing data: payments are handled by Stripe. We store your Stripe customer ID and subscription status. We never see or store your card number.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We don't use tracking or analytics cookies. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in.
  • We don't read your notes except as strictly required to operate the service (e.g. debugging a problem you report).

Third parties we rely on

  • Stripe for payments.
  • Resend for transactional email (password resets).
  • Open Library for book metadata — your search terms and scanned ISBNs are sent there to look up books; your account is not.
  • Our hosting provider, which stores the database and encrypted backups.

Your data, your call

You can export your full library as CSV at any time from Settings, and you can delete your account (and everything in it, permanently) from Settings too. Deletion also cancels any active subscription.
